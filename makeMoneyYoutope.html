1. Google AdSense

This is the most common method. When your channel joins the YouTube Partner Program, Google displays ads on your videos. Every time people view or click on those ads you earn money. Most new YouTubers start there.

YouTube Partner Program Terms

  • 1000 subscribers
  • 4000 Watch Hours or Shorts Views qualify
  • Adherence to all YouTube policies
  • AdSense Account

2. Affiliate Marketing

This is the most useful method. You describe a product or service. Then you enter the description of the affiliate link. When someone buys something you get a commission. Many people earn more money from affiliate marketing than from AdSense.

For example if you describe hosting, AI Tools or software, you can earn hundreds of dollars per sale.

3. Sponsorship

As your channel becomes popular companies will contact you. They pay you to talk about their products. Sometimes a single video can fetch hundreds or thousands of dollars.

4. Selling Digital Products

You can sell:

  • Ebooks
  • Courses
  • Templates
  • Canva Designs
  • Prompt Packs

This method is very profitable because you make the product once and then you can sell it many times.

Make Quality Videos

YouTube's algorithm favors videos that people watch until the end. That increases the Watch Time. Watch Time is one of the most important factors in YouTube Ranking.

How Does the YouTube Algorithm Work?

Many people think that YouTube randomly chooses which videos to show people. The truth is not like that. YouTube uses a very advanced algorithm that analyzes the behavior of people who use the platform. His goal is to show everyone his most watched videos. If your video gets people to stay on YouTube for a long time, the Algorithm starts showing your video to more people. That's why it's important to make a quality video that compels people to watch until the end.

The Most Important Things Algorithms Look For

  • Click Through Rate (CTR)
  • Average View Duration
  • Audience Retention
  • Watch Time
  • Likes and Comments
  • Shares
  • Subscriber Growth
  • Session Time

When all these points are high, YouTube understands that your video is useful, and starts recommending it on the Home Page and the Suggested Videos section.

What is CTR?

CTR (Click Through Rate) is the percentage of people who click on your video after seeing the thumbnail and title. For example: If 1000 people see your video and 100 people click, your CTR is 10%. A high CTR shows the algorithm that your title and thumbnail are attractive.

How to Increase CTR

  • Use high quality thumbnails.
  • Write a title for interested people.
  • Do not use a misleading title.
  • Use bright colors.
  • Write one clear message in the Thumbnail.

Watch Time Is The Key To Success

Watch Time is the total time people spend watching your videos. This is one of the most important parameters of YouTube. If someone watches your video for a long time, YouTube understands that your content is valuable. So he passes it on to other people.

How to Increase Watch Time

  • Start the scene using a strong Hook.
  • Don't go on with useless introductions.
  • Use stories.
  • Add different scenes.
  • Always tell people what they will benefit from the article or video.

What is the difference between CPM and RPM?

Two words are very common on YouTube. CPM and RPM. Many people get it wrong.

CPM

CPM is the amount that companies pay for 1000 impressions of an ad. This is not the money that the YouTuber gets in hand.

RPM

RPM is the actual money a YouTuber gets after YouTube takes their share. This is an important measure of your income.
