How to Make $100/Day Using AI (Step-by-Step Guide 2026)

If you want to make real money online, AI (Artificial Intelligence) has become a huge opportunity today. Many people are already making $50 to $200 a day using tools like ChatGPT, Fiverr, and other platforms.

In this article I will give you a clear system to make $100 a day without any prior experience. You only need internet, time, and desire to learn.

---

What Makes AI a Golden Opportunity in 2026?

AI has changed how online jobs are done. Instead of spending hours on a task, AI helps you complete it in minutes.

  • Automation of many tasks
  • Save time
  • High productivity
  • New money opportunities
---

Step 1: Choose a Money Making Skill

To reach $100/day, you must choose a skill that works in the market.

Best Skills:

  • Article Writing
  • Copywriting
  • Logo Design
  • Social Media Content
  • Video Script Writing

💡 Tip: Choose one skill that you are good at, then expand on it.

---

Step 2: Use the Right AI Tools

AI tools are the secret to success. These are the best:

  • ChatGPT – Writing content
  • Canva - Design
  • Midjourney – Images
  • CapCut - Video editing

By using these tools, you can do work 5x faster than a normal person.

---

Step 3: Create a Fiverr Account

Fiverr is the easiest place to find clients.

  1. Create a professional profile
  2. Add a description with SEO
  3. Create a gig with the right keyword
---

Step 4: Create a Selling Gig

Your gig should be attractive.

Example Gig Title:

"I will write SEO blog posts using AI (ChatGPT)"

Key Features:

  • Beautiful thumbnail
  • Clear description
  • Correct keywords
  • Competitive price
---

Step 5: How To Reach $100/Day

Example:

  • $10 gig × 10 orders = $100
  • $20 gig × 5 orders = $100

Start with a low price, after getting reviews increase the price.

---

Advanced Strategy (PRO Level)

If you want to exceed $100/day:

  • Add more gigs
  • Do upsells
  • Work with repeat clients
---

Mistakes People Make

  • A non-optimized gig
  • Wrong keywords
  • Lack of consistency
