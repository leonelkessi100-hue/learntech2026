How to Create Beautiful Pictures Using AI

Artificial Intelligence (AI) technology has in recent years dramatically changed the way people create images, designs and digital art. What used to require advanced skills in Photoshop or other software can today be done in minutes using AI Image Generators.

Many young Englishs are searching the internet today for terms such as "AI image generator", "AI photo", "ChatGPT photo", "Midjourney tutorial", "AI photo editing", and "how to make beautiful photos". The main reason is that AI gives everyone the ability to create high-quality images even if they have no design experience.

In this article you will learn how to create beautiful images using AI, the best tools to use, how to write powerful prompts, and techniques to help you get professional looking images.

What is AI Image Generator?

AI Image Generator is a software or online service that uses artificial intelligence to create an image from a text or idea provided by a user.

For example if you write:

"A modern English businessman standing in Mogadishu city at sunset, cinematic lighting, ultra realistic."

AI can turn that text into a high-quality image in minutes.

This technology is called Text-to-Image Generation.

Why AI Photos Are So Popular

  • It is easy to use.
  • No advanced design skills are required.
  • It saves a lot of time.
  • It reduces the cost of designers.
  • It produces high quality images.
  • It is suitable for blogging and content creation.
  • It is good for digital marketing.

How to Write a Strong Prompt

A prompt is a command you give to the AI.

A good prompt is more important than the software itself.

Best model:

Subject + Environment + Lighting + Style + Quality

Poor Prompt Example

"Make a picture of a man."

Example of a Strong Prompt

"A young English entrepreneur working on a laptop in a modern office, natural sunlight, ultra realistic photography, 8K quality, cinematic style."

This prompt gives the AI more information to create a beautiful image.
