Best Fiverr Gigs You Can Start Using AI (2026 Guide)

If you want to make money online without much experience, AI (Artificial Intelligence) has become a golden opportunity today. Fiverr is one of the best places to sell such services.

In this post we will learn the most profitable gigs you can start using AI tools such as ChatGPT, Midjourney and others.

---

What Makes Fiverr and AI a Great Opportunity?

Fiverr is a global marketplace where you can sell your digital skills. And AI makes you do work faster and with higher quality.

  • Less time = more money
  • A little experience is enough
  • Automation = easy work
---

1. AI Article Writing

One of the easiest gigs is article writing. Many people are looking for SEO optimized blog posts.

How is it done?

  • Use ChatGPT to write an article
  • Add SEO keywords
  • Sell on Fiverr

💡 Tip: Target keywords like "SEO blog post writer"

---

2. AI Logo Design

Many businesses need logos. AI tools like Midjourney or DALL·E help you make beautiful designs.

What do you need?

  • AI design tool
  • Basic editing (Canva)
---

3. Social Media Content Creation

Businesses need regular posts. Using AI you can:

  • Instagram captions
  • Facebook posts
  • TikTok scripts
---

4. AI Voice Over

AI voice tools allow you to create a professional voice without an expensive microphone.

---

5. Video Script Writing

YouTubers and TikTok creators need scripts. AI makes it easy for you.

---

6. Resume & CV Writing

Many people want a good CV. AI can help you create a professional CV.

---

7. AI Translation Services

If you know two languages, you can use AI to provide translation services.

---

How to Start a Fiverr Account?

  1. Create a Fiverr account
  2. Choose a niche
  3. Create an optimized gig
  4. Add a description with SEO
