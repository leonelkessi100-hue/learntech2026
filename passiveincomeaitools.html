What is Passive Income?

Passive income is money you earn without directly working every day. For example, if you write one article and it brings you visitors for a long time, that article can be a source of passive income.

The difference between active income and passive income:

  • Active income: you get paid when you work
  • Passive income: you earn money after the system works for you
