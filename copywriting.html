By LearnTech • March 2026 • 5 min read

What's different?

Both are written, but one of them understands copywriting.

Copywriting doesn't have to use big words, a lot of English or very beautiful sentences. Copywriting is a skill for your message written so that it is easy for the person to understand what is being offered, why it is important and the next step he can take.

So the question "What is copywriting?" It has a bigger answer than "advertising."

You can find copywriting on websites, advertisements, emails, landing pages, product descriptions, social media, sales pages, apps and many places where the business can talk to people.

What's even more interesting is that this skill can be made digital skill to make money.

What Is Copywriting?

Copywriting is writing a text for to motivate a person to do a certain action.

That action can be:

  • To buy a product.
  • To order a service.
  • To sign up for email.
  • To visit a website.
  • To request a demo.
  • To fill out a form.
  • To download the app.
  • To share the content with someone else.

Therefore copywriting combines:

Writing + Audience Understanding + Profitability + Persuasion + Action

For example, a company that sells software has two models and she can write her message.

Standard Writing

"Our software has a dashboard, analytics and automation."

Copywriting

"See your business data in one place, reduce manual work, and make reporting easy to understand.”

The second text tries to show the person benefit, is not the only feature.

And Google Ads guidance recommends that the ad copy should focus user needs and benefits, and the message should be clear, real and not overly push the person.

Who Is A Copywriter?

A copywriter is someone who writes copy for communication, marketing or sales.

A copywriter can work for:

  • Company
  • Agency
  • Startup
  • Website
  • E-commerce business
  • Freelancer
  • Personal brand

A good copywriter is not someone who writes a lot of words.

He is a person who understands:

  • The person he is talking to.
  • A person's problem.
  • A person's desire.
  • The benefit of the product.
  • It prevents him from buying.
  • The next step.

These two skills are very similar, but in their purpose are often different.

Content Writing

Content writing usually focuses on:

  • Teaching
  • Information
  • SEO traffic
  • Guides
  • Articles
  • Educational content

Copywriting

Copywriting usually focuses on:

  • Persuasion
  • Conversion
  • Sales
  • Leads
  • Action

Example article:

"How to Learn SEO?"

It can be content writing.

But a button with:

"Start SEO Course Today"

It's copywriting.

Both skills can help each other. A content writer and the copywriting site can make the article one that brings traffic and forwards it to the action reader.

Research: Copywriting Secrets Many People Forget

A good copywriter doesn't always start typing.

He starts research.

Before you write:

  • Know the product.
  • Know your audience.
  • Know your competitors.
  • Identify the problem.
  • Know the objections.
  • Know the desired outcome.

Questions You Can Ask Yourself

  • Who will buy this product?
  • What does she have now?
  • What do you want?
  • What is she afraid of?
  • What prevents her from buying?
  • What makes this product different from alternatives?

Once these questions are understood, the text will disappear "beautiful words" and becomes a well-targeted message.

Persuasion: How Does Copywriting Affect Decisions?

Persuasion does not mean forcing someone to do something.

You should clearly help him understand:

  • What is being given?
  • Why is it important to him?
  • What is the proof?
  • What's the next step?

Feature vs Benefit

Features: "The laptop has 16GB of RAM."

Benefit: “You can easily run multiple apps without it Always close other programs.”

A good copywriter translates the feature what the customer is benefiting from.

What is a Headline and Why is it Important?

Headline is the title or the first sentence that attracts attention one's feelings.

The headline gives the person a reason to read on.

A Weak Headline

"We Have a Website Service."

A Clearer Headline

"Build a Professional Website Ready for Mobile and Desktop."

A good headline tells the reader:

  • It is given.
  • The right person.
  • The benefit.
  • Sometimes the outcome.

Google Ads guidance also recommends that messaging communicate the needs of the user and relevant keywords, with the message is natural.

Copywriting Framework: How You Can Build Your Text

A copywriter can use different frameworks. A simple one that a beginner can understand is:

Problem → Agitate → Solution → Benefit → Proof → CTA

Problem

Show the audience's problem.

Agitate

Explain why the problem is important.

Solution

Present the solution.

Benefit

Explain what the user gets out of it.

Proof

When you have it, use evidence, examples or testimonials true.

CTA

Tell them the next step.

What is a CTA?

CTA or Call To Action it is a person's message telling them the next step.

Examples:

  • Get Started Now
  • Learn More
  • Order a Demo
  • Download the guide
  • Contact Our Team
  • Get a Quote

A good CTA is clear.

Instead of writing: "Submit"

you can use: "Get Your Value" if that is the right action.

Google Ads also recommends specific calls to action instead of using a very generic CTA.

What Is SEO Copywriting?

SEO copywriting is a combination of writing that makes people understand a topic and search-engine-friendly design.

But SEO copywriting is not keyword stuffing.

You need:

  • Search intent
  • Relevant keywords
  • Clear headings
  • Useful information
  • Internal links
  • Readable structure
  • Strong title
  • Good meta description

Google recommends content made for people, helpful, reliable and people-first, rather than content to be written in search engine only.

External: Google – Creating Helpful, Reliable, People-First Content

  • What's the next step?

Upwork Copywriting: How to Get Started?

Upwork can be your marketplace to find clients if you meet the requirements of the platform and have the skill and A portfolio you can work with.

Build Your Profile As A Copywriter

Do not write:

"I am a hardworking person and I can do many things."

Make it:

"I write conversion-focused website, landing page and email copy for businesses that want clearer messaging.”

Upwork specifically recommends that your profile be transparent primary expertise instead of the freelancer trying to Write all skills in one place.

How Can A Copywriter Write A Proposal?

Start the proposal with:

  • You understand.
  • The problem you saw.
  • Your approach.
  • Relevant sample.
  • An important question.

The Upwork proposal allows you to explain what you can do, ask questions, suggest next steps, and add work samples.

Don't Write the Same Proposal for Every Client

If the client needs an email copy, don't send them a proposal about logo design.

Relevance is important.

How Is Copywriting Evaluated?

A copywriter can evaluate the service in different ways.

Per Word

The text is judged by the number of words.

This method may be suitable for some writing tasks, but conversion-focused copywriting is always word count It does not represent the value.

Per Project

You can set a specific value for:

  • Landing page
  • Homepage
  • Email sequence
  • Ad package
  • Product descriptions

Hourly

You can get paid for the time you put into the project.

Retainer

The client may agree to receive monthly payments from you quantity or copywriting package.

Upwork currently states Freelancer Service Fee ranges 0% to 15% per contract, with the exact percentage shown when making a proposal or offer.

So the freelancer has to factor in the fee the platform when setting the price.

Cold Outreach: How to Show Business to Your Service?

Cold outreach is when you contact a business or potential client without having a relationship with them before.

Your outreach text should not:

"Hello sir, I am expert copywriter. Hire me."

Make it problem-focused:

“I looked at your homepage and saw that the headline does not clearly state who the service is for. I you made a short sample showing how the message can be proven."

This is not spam.

The aim is to show: research + relevance + value.

How to Edit Copy

Once the first draft is complete, don't rush to publish.

Question 1

Can someone understand what I'm saying in 5 seconds?

Question 2

Am I talking about the customer or am I talking about myself?

Question 3

Is the benefit clear?

Question 4

Is there a CTA?

Question 5

Are there any sentences that don't add up?

Good copywriting is often more obvious than when Remove words that don't do the job.

Is Good Copywriting Fake?

No.

Persuasion and deception are two different things.

Don't:

  • Fake testimonials
  • Fake scarcity
  • Fake guarantees
  • Claims without proof
  • Results not confirmed

The FTC insists that endorsements and testimonials be truthful, and there are disclosures when financial relationships or material connection that has an effect on the recommendation.

This is also important for affiliate copywriting. The reader should be able to understand what the recommendation says commercial relationship.

15 Mistakes New Copywriters Make

1. They Think Copywriting Is Just Grammar

Grammar is important, but persuasion and clarity are also important.

2. They Talk About the Product Only

The customer wants to understand the benefit.

3. They Don't Do Research

An essay without research can be generic.

4. A Weak Headline

If the opening is unauthenticated, the reader exits.

5. Very Long Sentences

Clarity is more important than complexity.

6. Features Only Listed

Turn the feature into a benefit.

7. No CTA

Don't leave the reader without knowing the next step.

8. Fake Testimonials

Don't fake proof.

9. Fake Results

Don't claim a result you can't verify.

10. No Portfolio

Make samples even if you are a beginner.

11. Each Client Proposal

The proposal is aligned with the project.

12. They Rate Per Word Only

Copywriting value is not always word count.

13. No Editing

The first draft usually needs correction.

14. They try to please everyone

A specific audience is better than a general message.

15. They're looking for a quick buck

A real skill requires practice and a portfolio.

FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions About Copywriting

What is copywriting?

Copywriting is writing messages intended for that person encourage him to take a specific action such as making a purchase, signup, click, inquiry or booking.

Who is the copywriter?

A copywriter is someone who writes copy for marketing, sales or communication.

Are copywriting and content writing the same thing?

No. Content writing often focuses on education, information or traffic, while copywriting usually concentrates persuasion and action.

Can Copywriting Make Money?

Yes. You can work in freelancing, agency, in-house, retain clients, personal brand and digital products.

Does copywriting require a degree?

A specific degree is not always the most important thing. Skill, portfolio, research, communication and ability you bring Quality work is important.

How to learn copywriting?

Learn fundamentals, headlines, benefits, persuasion, research, editing, SEO, email, ads and landing pages; then do it projects and portfolios.

How does a beginner copywriter make a portfolio?

You can create spec samples like landing pages, emails, ad copy, product descriptions and website copy even without you still not found a client.

Can Copywriting Work on Upwork?

How much is the Upwork copywriter fee?

Upwork now states Freelancer Service Fees 0% and 15% per contract, with the exact fee shown the relevant proposal or offer.

Can a copywriter make a freelance career?

Yes. When you build skills, portfolio, work history and client relations, copywriting can be freelance a career or business service.

What is SEO copywriting?

SEO copywriting is writing copy that serves the user at the same time designed for the search engine to understand the page, considering search intent, keywords, structure and readability.

Will AI replace copywriters?

Is copywriting suitable for beginners?

Yes. Beginner can start with practice, spec samples and portfolio, then he can move on to client work The skill gets stronger.
