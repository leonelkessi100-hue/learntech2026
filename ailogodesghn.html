Part 1: Understanding AI Design (The Fundamentals of Success)

What is AI Design?

AI Design is the use of artificial intelligence to create or facilitate design such as:

  • Logo design
  • Website UI/UX
  • Mobile app interfaces
  • Social media graphics
  • Brand identity

Instead of starting with a blank canvas, AI suggests ideas, layouts, and even complete designs.

How Does AI Work in Design?

AI tools use:

  • Machine learning
  • Smart templates
  • Design patterns that have already been trained

This makes a design that would take you 2 hours → done in 5 minutes.
