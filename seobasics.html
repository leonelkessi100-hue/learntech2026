Understanding SEO (Search Engine Optimization)

By LearnTech • February 2026 • 5 min read

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the most important technique that the blogger uses to make his website Make it available on Google, Bing, and other search engines. Good SEO increases traffic, it motivates readers, and helps the blog to be professional.

1. Understanding Keywords

Keywords are the words that users search for on Google. Before writing an article, do some keyword research to find out what people are looking for. For example: if you are talking about blogging, Keywords like "how to start a blog" or "SEO for beginners" is important.

2. Title & Meta Description

The title and meta description are the first thing for Google and readers to see. Your title should be clear, include keywords, and attractive at the same time. The meta description should be brief (150-160 characters), while explaining the content of the article.

3. Content Quality

Your content should be **original**, deep, in response to reader questions. Avoid copy-paste, and use headings, bullet points, and images to improve readability.

4. Internal & External Links

  • Internal links: Link to your other articles to make navigation easier for the reader.
  • External links: Link to authoritative websites to increase your SEO.

5. Images Optimization

Images should have alt text that explains the content of the image. It is also important to keep the image size small so that the page loads quickly.

6. Mobile-Friendly Design

Google values mobile-friendly websites. Make sure your blog looks good on mobiles, tablets, and desktop, while navigation is easy.

Important tip: SEO is an **ongoing journey**, not a one-time event. Add new keywords to new articles, build backlinks, Update your content and monitor Google Analytics to find out what your readers like.
