If you are a person who wants to start an online business and revive his financial career, you must have heard of or are looking for the most profitable programs known as Affiliate Marketing.

Many people starting out in international marketing are confused by websites that offer very low commissions or difficult products to sell to people. However, there is a great secret used by professionals, which is software marketing called SaaS (Software as a Service). Every platform that sells software needs marketing, but instead of asking each company for work separately, there is a big bridge that connects all those companies and you. That bridge is called PartnerStack.

In this long and detailed article, we will take a deep look at everything about PartnerStack, from how to create a successful account, how to break the acceptance algorithm, to the easiest ways to bring traffic or foreign visitors to these programs from anywhere in the world. Be sure to read the article to the end because each section contains golden knowledge that can make a difference in your financial future.

What is PartnerStack? General Understanding of the Web

PartnerStack is not an ordinary company that sells one thing, but a global platform (B2B Partnership Platform) designed to connect companies that produce modern software and marketers (Affiliate Marketers, influencers, and agencies). Major software companies such as Monday.com, Notion, Webflow, and Linktrack prefer to run their affiliate programs on PartnerStack because its accounting system and incentive engines are more sophisticated.

When you sign up for PartnerStack, you get access to hundreds of different companies that offer different services. This makes it easy for you to manage all your ad links, earnings, and follower reports in one place. Instead of opening an account in 50 different places, PartnerStack brings you all those companies in one clean dashboard.

Link Between PartnerStack AND Other Networks Like ClickBank Or Amazon

To understand the power of PartnerStack, we have to compare it with other popular networks. Amazon Associates, for example, pays a very small commission of between 1% and 5% because they sell physical products that cost more to produce and ship. Also, ClickBank is mostly known for e-books and health levers, which are sometimes difficult to convince people.

PartnerStack is particularly limited SaaS (Software as a Service). The software has no shipping or physical storage costs. When a person buys software, the company can automatically generate a net profit of up to 90%. Therefore, software companies are willing to give you a very high commission of 20%, 30%, or even 50% of recurring revenue every month.

Best Ways To Make Money With PartnerStack

Within PartnerStack there isn't just one way to make money; There are several systems that vary in their ease and effectiveness. Successful marketers use two main systems: Cost Per Lead (CPL) and Cost Per Action (CPA). Let's analyze these two systems one by one.

1. Cost Per Lead (CPL) or Pay-Per-Sign-Up

This is the easiest and most suitable program for anyone who wants to start earning fast. Many companies on PartnerStack do not require the customers you bring to pay first. They just want a person to sign up for their Free Trial Signup to get their email. The company knows that a few percent of those people will buy the service in the future.

Note: PartnerStack includes programs that provide you with $35 to $50+ only when a real person verifies his email (Qualified Lead). This is why marketing these programs is a golden opportunity that cannot be compared to other programs.

2. Recurring Commission Programs

This is the long term goal of anyone who wants to build passive income. When you hire software for a fixed monthly fee (such as project management or marketing tools), the company pays you a percentage of the monthly revenue. As long as that person is a customer of the company and pays the monthly fee, your money will be automatically deposited into your account every month without you having to do anything else.

How To Beat The PartnerStack Algorithm And Get Official Acceptance

The biggest problem for new people is that they register on the website and then get rejected quickly. This happens because PartnerStack monitors the quality of incoming marketers and blocks people who want to spam or have no official sources of traffic. To get through this process, you need to show that you are a professional with a clear plan.

Choosing a Brand Name and Building a Website

When applying, the first thing they look at is where you will advertise their products. Having an official website about technology, ie LearnTech, is your greatest weapon. Even though your website is written in English, PartnerStack managers consider you to be an expert in building websites with a specific market (Local Market SEO) to manage. They look at the overall design (Layout), that it is in Google (Google Indexed), and that it is working correctly.

Application Preparation: Writing Introductory Essays

To gain 100% credibility, you need to publish quality articles on your website that explain their website or software they are famous for. When the support person sees that your website already contains a scientific explanation of their system, he knows that you are serious about the job and did not apply incorrectly. This removes the doubt of the authorities in terms of acceptance.

The Most Effective Strategies to Bring Foreign Traffic to PartnerStack and Fiverr

Once you are approved for the network, the second hurdle you face is: Where are you getting people to buy or sign up for the software? Since these programs are international (English), you need residents of the United States, Europe, or Canada to generate income. Many people think that if they are in Englisha or outside the West they cannot reach those people, but that is wrong.

1. YouTube Shorts: The King of International Traffic

YouTube Shorts is the most powerful tool that does not depend on your physical location (No SIM-card location restriction). The YouTube algorithm looks at the topic (Topic) and the language in which your video is written. If you create a new Channel about technology devices (Example: TechStack Guide or DevSmart Solutions), by uploading videos in English, YouTube directly appeals to people in America and Europe who are interested in tech.

The best way to use Shorts on PartnerStack and Fiverr programs is to show people a quick solution. For example, you can record your screen using software that offers a free trial, and write a script that says: "Stop wasting hours organizing your tasks manually. This tool automates everything for free. Check the pinned comment for the link!". Anyone who clicks on your link in the first comment (Pinned Comment), will generate income for you.

2. Breaking the TikTok Algorithm for Foreigners

TikTok is a huge traffic site, but it uses your SIM card to track your location. If you want to use TikTok in the West, you must completely remove the local SIM card from your phone, and then use a strong VPN (Paid Premium VPN) connected to the United States before opening a new TikTok account. This forces TikTok to target your video to young people in those areas.

The Best Seasons for Uploading Videos to Get Westerners

The time you publish the video or article has a big impact on your success. Since you want people in the US and Europe, you have to take into account the time they wake up or leave work. The best time to put your shorts on is in between 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM East African Time (EAT). This time coincides with the afternoon when Europeans leave work and the early morning when Americans take their lunch break.
